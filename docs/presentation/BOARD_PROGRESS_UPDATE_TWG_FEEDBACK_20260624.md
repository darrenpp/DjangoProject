# Progress Update on PNG Nursing Council and Medical Board Online Regulatory Workforce Platform

Date: 24 June 2026  
Prepared for: Board, Technical Working Group, NDOH management, registrar leadership, and project stakeholders  
Project: PNG Nursing Council and Medical Board Online Regulatory Workforce Platform

## 1. Executive Summary

The PNG Nursing Council and Medical Board Online Regulatory Workforce Platform has progressed from a database-viewing tool into a controlled regulatory operations platform. The system now supports structured registry management, role-separated board dashboards, application workflow controls, Nursing Council analytics, receipt review, records management, complaints and discipline modules, regulatory decision tracking, NHWA reporting, public guidance, and data-quality review.

The recent platform presentation was successfully completed. The project team is now awaiting feedback, comments, and formal direction from the Technical Working Group (TWG). This feedback is required to confirm the next priorities for user acceptance testing, production hosting, security approval, policy and SOP sign-off, data ownership, and remaining data-quality work.

The platform foundation is operational in the local/testing environment. Production launch has not yet been declared complete and remains dependent on formal UAT, hosting/security approval, data-owner sign-off, SOP approval, and controlled production rollout decisions.

## 2. Current Progress

### 2.1 Platform Governance and Access

The platform now separates Nursing Council and Medical Board workspaces through role-based access and board-specific dashboards. This supports regulatory privacy and helps ensure users view and act only on the records and workflows relevant to their authorised board or office scope.

The system includes staff-facing dashboards, reviewer workflows, public guidance pages, practitioner-facing portals, and controlled access for finance, data-quality, registrar, reviewer, and system administration users.

### 2.2 Nursing Council Analytics Snapshot

The Nursing Council dashboard now uses an active cleansed analytics snapshot to support workforce and licence reporting without automatically converting imported workbook rows into legal registry person records.

Current verified Nursing Council analytics figures from the June 2026 documentation are:

| Indicator | Current Figure |
|---|---:|
| Nursing Council analytics lifecycle records | 34,851 |
| Clean ATP records | 19,998 |
| Clean provisional records | 8,158 |
| Clean full-licence records | 6,695 |
| Estimated practitioner match groups | 22,765 |
| Data quality health score | 87.0% |

The system deliberately keeps cleansed workbook analytics separate from the legal practitioner registry until records are verified, matched, reviewed, and promoted through approved workflows.

### 2.3 Records, Data Quality, and Review Tools

The Records Hub and registrar-facing record tables now support structured operational review, including search, sorting, pagination, view actions, edit actions where authorised, and add-new actions where role permissions allow.

The duplicate review queue supports grouped source-row review and staff actions for reviewing, merging, and reopening possible duplicate records. Missing-data review and duplicate-review functions are available to support data-quality cleanup before management statistics are used for formal reporting.

### 2.4 Receipts and Finance Reporting

Receipt-owner linking has been implemented as a controlled evidence-matching process. Receipts are linked only where supporting evidence is strong, such as receipt number, name, registration number, practitioner number, source row, amount, or date alignment.

Unmatched, duplicate, suspicious, weakly matched, or high-value receipts remain review items. Receipts are treated as finance evidence and must not be counted as practitioners.

Current June 2026 evidence includes:

| Area | Current Figure |
|---|---:|
| Receipt records | 11,501 |
| Receipt amount recorded | PGK 822,882.30 |

Finance reporting remains separated by office scope for Nursing Council and Medical Board reporting.

### 2.5 Regulatory Operations Modules

The platform now includes modules needed for a more complete regulatory operating environment:

- Records Hub for structured staff record management.
- Duplicate review queue for possible duplicate records.
- Missing-data review tools for data-quality cleanup.
- Financial forecast and receipt reporting by office scope.
- Document repository functions with controlled document handling.
- ICMS complaints register.
- Discipline case register.
- Regulatory decision register.
- NHWA workbook centre for reporting alignment.
- Public FAQ, public/practitioner forum, and mapped reference records.
- Staff notifications, mailbox status, and read/opened message tracking.

These modules support the movement from basic data storage toward auditable regulatory workflow and governance.

## 3. TWG Feedback Status

The platform presentation has been successfully completed. The project team is currently waiting for the TWG team to provide formal feedback, comments, and direction.

TWG feedback is needed to confirm:

- Which items should be prioritised before UAT.
- Who will act as Nursing Council and Medical Board data owners.
- Which legacy workbook or data extract should be treated as the official starting snapshot for each board.
- How duplicate, missing-data, receipt mismatch, and legacy workbook review items should be prioritised.
- The preferred production hosting and security approval pathway.
- The SOP and policy sign-off path for registry decisions, complaints, discipline, document control, and production operations.
- The target date for UAT completion and launch-readiness review.

Until this feedback is received and recorded, the project should remain in controlled testing, refinement, and readiness-preparation status.

## 4. Current Status

The system foundation is operational in the local/testing environment and can demonstrate the major platform functions required by the Nursing Council and Medical Board.

The following areas are considered substantially progressed:

- Role-separated dashboards for Nursing Council and Medical Board users.
- Active Nursing Council analytics snapshot and dashboard reporting.
- Data-quality visibility through missing-data and duplicate-review tools.
- Controlled receipt-linking and finance reporting.
- Records Hub and staff record-management interfaces.

## 5. Key Risks and Controls

| Risk | Current Control / Required Action |
|---|---|
| Legacy workbook records may not represent verified legal practitioner identities. | Keep analytics snapshot separate from legal registry person records until controlled promotion is approved. |
| Duplicate and missing legacy data may affect management statistics. | Continue duplicate-review and missing-data cleanup before formal publication of figures. |
| Receipts may be weakly matched, duplicated, suspicious, or high value. | Keep review workflow active and link receipts only where evidence is strong. |
| Production launch could proceed before governance sign-off. | Maintain testing/readiness status until UAT, hosting, security, SOP, and data-owner approvals are complete. |
| Public map and reference records may require verified coordinates. | Complete verified coordinate review before relying on map outputs for public demonstration. |

## 7. Conclusion

The platform has reached a strong functional foundation for regulatory operations, reporting, and governance.
