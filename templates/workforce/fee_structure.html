Required Documents
  • Recent passport-size photo
  • Academic certificate / transcript
  • National ID or Birth Certificate
  • Competency statement (G4/G5 for graduates)
  • Payment receipt
  • Police clearance (for new registrations)
  • Logbook / Practical record (where applicable)
Registration & Renewal Guidelines – Step-by-Step Process
  1. Create an account using your email and license number (if available)
  2. Select the correct form code (NC1, NC2, NC3, G1, G4, G5, etc.)
  3. Fill in all required personal and professional details
  4. Upload all required documents
  5. Pay the applicable fee and attach the receipt
  6. Submit the application for review
  7. Receive email notification once approved or rejected
Important Notes
  • All applications are reviewed by the Registrar / Reviewer team
  • Provisional Licence (NC1) is valid for 6 months only
  • Renewal (NC3) must be done before expiry to avoid late fees
  • Graduands must complete G1 before applying for full licence
  • Medical Board applications use the dedicated Medical Board registration workflow.
