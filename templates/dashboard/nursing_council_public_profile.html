Legislative Mandate

The Papua New Guinea Nursing Council (PNGNC) was established in 1964 and is mandated under the Medical Registration Act 1980 to regulate nursing and midwifery education, registration, licensing and scope of practice in Papua New Guinea.

The Council is responsible for protecting public health and safety by ensuring that nurses and midwives meet approved standards of competence, professional conduct and professional practice.

In carrying out its statutory mandate, the Council exercises regulatory oversight and authority over matters relating to nursing and midwifery education, registration, licensing, accreditation, professional standards, professional conduct and scope of practice in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Key Functions

Registration

Register and maintain a current register of qualified nurses and midwives in Papua New Guinea.

Licensing

License eligible practitioners to undertake nursing and midwifery scope of practice in Papua New Guinea.

Standards

Set and enforce professional standards, codes of conduct and practice guidelines.

Accreditation

Accredit and monitor nursing and midwifery education programs.

Complaints Management

Receive, assess and manage complaints to ensure professional accountability.

Discipline

Investigate misconduct and take appropriate disciplinary action.

Policy & Advice

Provide policy advice and input to strengthen nursing and midwifery regulation.

Partnerships

Collaborate with stakeholders to strengthen health systems and workforce development.

Our Role & Responsibility

The PNG Nursing Council is the independent statutory authority responsible for regulating nursing and midwifery professions. We act in the public interest by:

Upholding professional standards and ethics.

Ensuring practitioners are competent and fit to undertake their approved scope of practice.

Promoting quality nursing and midwifery education.

Protecting the public from harm.

Strengthening regulatory systems and governance.

Supporting a safe, skilled and sustainable workforce.

Advancing the profession for better health outcomes.

Strong Regulation. Safe Practice. Quality Care. Healthy People. Our Vision. Our Mandate. Our Commitment.
