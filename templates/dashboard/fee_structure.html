Payment Methods & Guidelines

Accepted Payment Methods
  • Cash payments at designated offices
  • Bank transfers to designated accounts
  • Mobile money transfers (available soon)
  • Credit/Debit cards (selected locations)
Payment Guidelines
Important: All payments must be made in Papua New Guinean Kina (PGK). Receipts must be kept for all transactions.
Refunds: Fees are non-refundable once services have been processed. Contact the relevant council/board for refund inquiries.
