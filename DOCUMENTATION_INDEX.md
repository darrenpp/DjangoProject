## Security Rules

- Nursing Council and Medical Board records must stay separated through backend role and office-scope checks, not only by hidden menu links.
- Finance users can view financial forecast and workforce flow only; they cannot perform CRUD operations or routine registrar approvals.
- Reviewer-style users remain locked out of full Nursing Council or Medical Board operations until their operational access request is approved by the relevant registrar or System Admin.
- Public register responses must never expose date of birth, phone, email, address, passport, police clearance, medical report, transcript, receipt image, or internal notes.
- `/admin/` is reserved for System Admin only.
- Temporary testing passwords must be changed before production launch.
- AI Staff Assistant is a guidance tool only. It must not replace registrar review, payment verification, source evidence, audit logs, or official decision records.
